# Starter music

The starter pack uses files supplied by Lynli for this private prototype. Audio is copied without transcoding. MP3 ID3 tags are filled in from release names; artwork is downloaded from official NCS release pages. All three metadata-bearing M4As preserve their embedded title, artist, album, and cover. Only KYOTO has a missing album field, which is set to KYOTO [NCS Release].

- HESTER — HESTER - KYOTO: https://ncs.io/kyoto
- gabriawll — Recall: https://ncs.io/recall
- 2frers — EYES ON US: https://ncs.io/EYESONUS

Release label: NoCopyrightSounds. NCS release-page artwork and supplied audio remain the property of their respective owners. Review the applicable music/artwork distribution terms before publishing or redistributing this prototype beyond the private repository.
