# Klang design

Home opens with My Mix, a compact typographic/artwork composition, followed by albums and recently added tracks. The Home title, device song count, Plugins link, Favorites/Songs shortcut cards and everyday add/shuffle action row are removed. A small settings control is retained. Import actions remain in Library and Settings, or in the empty-library/access state where they are relevant.

My Mix automatically includes every available local song. The Home preview and dedicated page share stretched two-line lettering, rotated pill artwork, circular secondary artwork and a morphing play control. The preview is compact; the full playlist gets the larger artwork composition. My Mix and Favorites are built-in collections. Library → Playlists lets people create their own playlists, open them, add songs in a searchable picker, rename them, reorder/remove songs from each row’s menu, and delete the playlist without deleting music. Every ordinary song menu also offers Add to playlist. IDs and ordering persist in a separate preferences file; missing tracks remain in the stored playlist for later rescans.

From Material 3: the display roles retain published 57/45/36sp sizes and 64/52/44sp line heights. Smaller type stays native Android. Surface/container and paired on-color roles organize the screens. Connected toggles, ShortNavigationBar, the expressive motion scheme, native ripples and accessible Slider interactions use Material components.

Klang design decisions: Roboto Flex display styles are bundled as fixed font instances at weight 900, width 125 and matching optical sizes. My Mix adds 1.12 horizontal text scaling. These are editorial choices inspired by Lynli’s reference, not the default M3 emphasized weight. Only the short display lettering gets this treatment. The compact collage is 216dp tall at normal font scaling; the full version is 404dp. Utility buttons use surfaceContainerHigh/onSurface. The stable app palette is monochrome by default, with optional Material You wallpaper colors. The Plugins settings row uses a rounded Extension symbol.

Cover colors default on under the new, scoped content preference. They apply to Now playing and album pages; Home, Search, Library, Settings and playlists retain the app palette. The mini player always uses the current cover’s Material palette independently. Palette plus MaterialKolor supplies complete HCT schemes and effects-spring color transitions. Dark app surfaces remain near-black and neutral.

Navigation stays inset over full-height content, with trailing list space keeping final rows accessible. Geometry uses fast spatial springs (0.6 damping / 800 stiffness) or slow spatial springs (0.8 / 200); color/opacity uses effects springs (1.0 / 800). Now playing allocates its remaining height to artwork and anchors transport controls near the bottom. The native Material Slider supplies seeking gestures and accessibility, with a custom animated squiggle track and round playhead. Large rounded tonal previous/next controls flank Play/Pause; shuffle/repeat sit beside elapsed/total time to keep the transport buttons spacious.

Reference screenshots guide composition, not assets. Artwork comes from the music files. Font, Spotify and Material icon attribution is in the credits. The Spotify placeholder remains unavailable in the Plugins page, reachable through Settings. LRCLIB is the first functional bundled provider; plugin details, buttons and settings come from its manifest. Minimum/target Android is API 36; compilation uses SDK 37.2.

Collection playback controls follow the source of the queue: play becomes pause only in the album or playlist that started it, and resumes at the existing position when paused. Stable collection IDs are carried in Media3 playlist metadata and saved/restored with the queue. A shared song in another playlist does not activate that playlist’s big button. Playback started from Home’s song rows, Search or Library does not claim a collection. The active album/playlist row uses the same animated playing indicator as other song rows, keeping its title spacious. The mini player stays available for transport and favorite actions; active-row artwork/title opens Now playing.

The mini player follows horizontal drags and springs into the adjacent song: left goes next, right goes previous. It uses Media3 adjacent-item commands, preserving paused playback and following shuffle/repeat order. The previous gesture always selects the previous item instead of the transport button's restart-after-three-seconds behavior. A downward drag only dismisses a paused player. Dismissal stops and clears the live queue and its collection metadata, cancels the sleep timer and removes the mini player; the service persists the empty queue so it stays dismissed after reopening. Each completed swipe rechecks the current track, and dismissal also rechecks playback intent so buffering/resumed playback cannot be cleared by an old gesture. Accessible previous/next/dismiss actions provide the same operations without swiping.

From Material 3: swipe feedback uses the theme's fast spatial spring. Klang gesture decisions: direction locks after native touch slop; completion uses 64dp horizontally or 48dp downward, or at least 20dp with an 800dp/s fling in the same direction. Horizontal queue boundaries apply light drag resistance and return to the original position; short, cancelled or upward drags do not change playback.

## Sound controls (0.5)

Now playing groups playlist, lyrics, devices and EQ icons above the seek bar. Lyrics becomes available when a lyrics plugin is enabled. The lyrics page uses the cover palette, large timed text, word filling only when word timestamps exist, spring scrolling and seek-on-line-tap. Top and transport controls hide after three seconds of inactivity and return on touch. See [lyrics design decisions](PLUGINS.md). Artwork colors fade from the top into the normal app surface at the bottom. The full equalizer page pairs a curve/preset card with native vertical centered sliders, in two groups of five selected through frequency-range tabs, and a single enable switch. Song/headphone assignments stay beneath the sound controls rather than introducing separate editing modes.

Long-pressing a song opens its row menu, and both that menu and the Now playing overflow expose a cover-themed properties sheet. The cover theme wraps the native Material modal, so its opaque surface, drag handle and content use the same palette. A compact song header leads into non-interactive Material segmented list items for metadata and file location. Unknown platform metadata is omitted or shown as a dash; an unavailable location stops showing a loading message. Playlist pills wrap, and the header and Close/Done actions remain outside the scrolling details.

From Material 3: the modal uses surface container low and the native accessible drag handle; segmented lists use their built-in shapes, gaps, padding and text/icon color roles. The song title uses emphasized title large, and buttons use native pressed-shape animation. Klang design decisions: the sheet retains the sleep timer's 32dp top corners and extra 10dp handle padding, uses a 64dp cover and 24dp horizontal content margins, and switches metadata values to supporting text when width divided by font scale is below 300dp. Hide from app is a secondary text action beside the filled Done button; a wrapping action row accommodates larger text.

Hiding is reversible and never touches the file: hidden ids are filtered at scan time and collected on Settings → Library → Hidden audio files. Settings ends with a neutral GitHub logo, lynlioss/klang and an external-link icon button. The Now playing overflow also holds go to artist/album and the sleep timer. The timer is a radial sheet instead of preset buttons: the ring maps every five minutes of a 0–180 minute range, the centre shows the animated value, and presets only act as shortcuts. An end timestamp drives the live countdown shown in the overflow and sheet.

Playing rows use a decorative four-bar looping indicator; it is not live audio metering. Favorite taps use a fast expressive spatial spring for scale/rotation, and motion follows the Android animator setting. Choreography amplitudes and the indicator period are app design decisions. Artwork state reuses cached images immediately; separate thumbnail/full-size memory caches and bounded disk storage prevent repeated metadata extraction on scrolling.

EQ opened from Now playing inherits the cover palette and slides over the entire player with expressive spatial/effects springs. Its surface extends behind the system bars; Settings EQ keeps the normal app theme.
